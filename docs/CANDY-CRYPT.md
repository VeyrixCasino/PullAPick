Sections are 10 layers. The odds stop moving at section 12 (layers 111-120, the Crypt's
bottom); the rock keeps hardening below it. The Crypt is remade every 20 minutes while the
event runs.

**Per-hour columns assume 1,200 blocks an hour** (a block every three seconds). That is
this document's own estimate, not a measurement: nobody has played the Crypt yet.

## 6. The Trick-or-Treat case

**4,000 candy.** Halloween pets and Halloween skins only. The server rolls the rarity, the
kind and which one, **then a 50/50 wheel: TREAT you get it, TRICK you get nothing.** A skin you
already own pays stardust instead. Kinds: pets 70%, skins 30%.
"Shown" is the chance the wheel shows that prize; "kept" is the chance you get it (shown x TREAT).

## 7. Halloween tools

Sixteen, one per candy ore, crafted from it at the Forge and never dropped. Prestige 1-10, paid in more
of that ore (step k costs craft x 0.5 x 1.2^(k-1), rounded up); each prestige adds +30% of the base power.
At most 10. Scrapping pays 50% of the candy value of every ore spent on the tool, no ore back.
New tools are crafted only while the event runs; prestige works after it too.
