# Skins — 200 concepts in 8 sets, plus a bonus set, F to SSS

**Status: PROPOSED (2026-10-10). Nothing is built.** This is the name/set/tier
roster for approval. Numbers and buffs are in the second half.

## How a skin works

- **200 concepts in the core eight sets**, plus a 25-strong **Halloween bonus
  set** that sits outside the count — 225 in all. Each concept exists for **all
  five tool classes**, same name, same set, same tier, same buffs, so 225
  designs is **1,125 skin instances**.
- **The skin is the damage BASE.** `damage = skinBase × oreDamage × everything else`.
  Ore tools are the floor; the skin is the ceiling and the chase.
- **Grades are the game's existing ladder** — `F D C B A S SS SSS`
  (`MineCards.GRADE_RANK`, rank 1–8). No E, because the game has no E.
- **The form may change per class, the identity may not.** A set's hammer can be
  an axe, its drill a laser, its explosive a canister — as long as it reads as
  the same object family. What must stay identical across the five is the
  palette, the material and the trim, because that is what makes a set
  recognisable in someone else's hands at twenty studs.

Per set: 25 concepts, weighted **F 6 · D 5 · C 4 · B 3 · A 3 · S 2 · SS 1 · SSS 1**.
One SSS per set — eight in the core sets, nine counting Halloween.

## Numbers — all PROPOSED

### A skin is a BUDGET, not a damage number

Owner, 2026-10-10: *"f starts at 50% budget (SHOULDN'T ONLY BE ORE DAMAGE..
THIS SHOULD BE BUFFS AND SHIT)"*.

So a skin carries two things: a **base multiplier** that the ore tool's damage
multiplies, and a **buff kit** bought with a points budget.

### Skins price on their OWN curve

Owner, 2026-10-10: *"KEEP IN MIND THESE BOOSTS EFFECT THE TOOL LIKE THEY ARE
BASE STATS.. SHOULD ALMOST HAVE ITS OWN BUDGET SYSTEM"*. Agreed, and done.

The arithmetic is still **points = `amount × 100 × weight`**, the same shape as
`MineStats.valueOf`, but the weights are **`MineSkins.WEIGHT`** and nothing
outside skins reads them. Two reasons; the second is the one that matters:

1. A skin stat lands on the tool like a base stat and applies to **every tool in
   the class** — a different thing to buy than the same number on one rune.
2. **It isolates the blast radius.** Retuning skins must not move runes, gear,
   traits, the skill tree or pets. Editing `MineStats.STATS` moves all of them
   at once and silently, because every one of those systems divides by those
   weights. `MineStats` is therefore left completely untouched by this feature.

**What the weights are priced against.** `MineCards.ADDITIVE_STATS` splits every
stat into two kinds that behave nothing alike once a player stacks sources:

- **Additive** — `procPower`, `blastChance`, `zap`, `tidalWave`, `earthquake`,
  `ricochet`, `blastDamage`, `tideDamage`, `zapDamage`, `shatterDamage`,
  `oreHaul`, `pulverize`, `fossilFind`. They accumulate **from 0 and sum**: a
  skin's +50% and a rune's +50% make +100%, spent once. A skin buying these is
  buying a slice of a shared pot. **Cheap.**
- **Multiplicative** — `swingRate`, `gemFind`, `rareOre`, `coinBonus`,
  `dirtBreak` and the luck sectors. They accumulate **from 1** into a
  multiplier, and `Dig.oreLuck` / `Dig.packLuck` then multiply that sector pool
  by the **global luck pool** on top. **Expensive** — and the luck sectors most
  of all, because they compound with every luck source a grinder already owns.

| stat | skin weight | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oreLuck` · `chestLuck` · `packLuck` | **2.6** | multiply with the global luck pool |
| `swingRate` | **2.4** | multiplies the skin's own base, and every swing source |
| `rareOre` | 1.6 | multiplicative, single channel |
| `gemFind` | **1.5** | owner, 2026-10-10: bring gem down to 1.5 |
| `procPower` | 1.4 | additive, but drives every proc at once |
| `blastChance` · `zap` · `tidalWave` | 1.3 | additive |
| `blastDamage` · `tideDamage` · `zapDamage` · `shatterDamage` | 1.2 | additive, one proc each |
| `earthquake` · `ricochet` · `fossilFind` | 1.2 | additive |
| `pulverize` | 1.1 | additive |
| `dirtBreak` · `oreHaul` | 1.0 | narrow, and `oreHaul` is capped by `ORE_HAUL_CAP` |
| `coinBonus` | 0.9 | multiplicative, weakest currency |

Global `luck` is not on this table at all — skins roll sectors only.

### The spread rule, which is what actually stops the degenerate build

**No stat may take more than 40% of a skin's budget, and no rolled stat less
than 10%.**

Without it the whole system collapses to one line: every serious player hunts
the one SSS that dumped all 500 points into `oreLuck`, and nothing else is worth
equipping. 500 points of pure `oreLuck` is **+192%**, which then multiplies the
global luck pool — a grinder sitting on +100% global luck would be at **5.8×**
ore cases from one item. At 40% it is +77%, strong but not the only answer, and
the remaining 300 points have to go somewhere a different build wants.

This is the rule to break first if skins feel too flat in play.

Owner, 2026-10-10: **F is 80 points, SSS is 500.** The ladder between them is a
flat ~1.30× per tier, so no rung is a dead step and the whole spread is 6.25×.

| Tier | base × | buff budget (pts) | buff slots |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F | 1.15 | **80** | 2 |
| D | 1.35 | 105 | 2 |
| C | 1.60 | 135 | 3 |
| B | 1.90 | 175 | 3 |
| A | 2.30 | 225 | 4 |
| S | 3.00 | 290 | 4 |
| SS | 4.00 | 380 | 5 |
| SSS | **5.50** | **500** | 5 |

**F opens at 80 points**, which is a real kit on its own. Read against the live
weights in `MineStats.STATS`, 80 points is any of:

| spend | weight | buys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all on Swing Rate | 1.4 | **+57%** |
| all on Coin Bonus | 0.8 | **+100%** |
| all on Gem Find | 1.8 | +44% |
| all on Luck | 2.0 | +40% |

That is the fix for the dead-F problem — an F skin is worth equipping for the
buffs before its 1.15× base is counted at all.

It also shows why the budget has to be spent in the game's own currency rather
than in flat percentages: the same 80 points is +100% of a cheap stat or +40%
of an expensive one, and that difference is the balance work already done in
`MineStats` weights. A skin system with its own percentages would quietly
undo it.

**The base ladder came DOWN from the 10× I proposed last time**, to 5.5×,
because the buff kit now carries part of the load and the two multiply. At 10×
base *plus* 500 points of buffs an SSS skin would be worth more than the entire
ore ladder beneath it. Say the word if you want 10× anyway and I will re-table
the budget down to compensate.

**For scale:** 500 points is roughly a full rune's worth of stats several times
over, so an SSS skin is a build-defining item rather than an upgrade. That is
the intent — it is the thing people chase — but it is the number to argue with
first if the game ends up feeling like skins are the only progression.

### What a skin may roll

Drawn from `MineStats.STAT_ORDER`, minus two groups that would pay **nothing**
on a tool:

- **`RUNE_ONLY`** — `blastRadius`, `reach`, `coolant`, `shortFuse`
- **`PET_ONLY`** — `mineSpeed`, `luckyFind`, `scrap`
- **plain `luck`** — see below. Luck is divided into sectors now, and the
  global is not one of them.

This is not a style preference. `MineCards.addStat` drops restricted keys, which
is exactly how 26 pets ended up carrying a blastRadius line that paid nothing.
A skin rolling one would read as a buff on the tile and do nothing in the mine.

**Luck is divided into sectors, and the global multiplies all of them.** This is
the one that would have done real damage if it had been rolled by mistake:

```
oreLuck  channel = b.luck × b.oreLuck      MineServer:6700
packLuck channel = b.luck × b.packLuck     MineServer:6707
chestLuck        = b.luck × b.chestLuck    MineServer:6693
```

`luck` is not a fourth sector sitting beside the other three — it is their
parent. A skin rolling it would buff every luck channel at once and compound
with each, so it is strictly better than any sector skin and stacks with all of
them. Skins roll the **sectors** only: `oreLuck`, `chestLuck`, `packLuck`,
`fossilFind`, `gemFind`, `rareOre`.

> Worth a separate look, not fixed here: `luck` is weight **2.0** while
> `oreLuck` and `packLuck` are **2.4** — so the stat that multiplies three
> channels is priced *below* the ones it multiplies. Every system spending a
> MineStats budget (runes, gear, traits, the tree) is affected, not just skins,
> which is why this is a note rather than an edit.

That leaves the live pool: `swingRate`, `zap`, `blastChance`, `tidalWave`,
`rareOre`, `gemFind`, `coinBonus`, `oreLuck`, `chestLuck`, `packLuck`,
`fossilFind`, `oreHaul`, `pulverize`, `earthquake`, `ricochet`, `procPower`,
`blastDamage`, `tideDamage`, `zapDamage`, `shatterDamage`, `dirtBreak`.

**Each set leans on its own stats**, so a set reads as a build and not a random
spread — and so collecting one is a decision:

**Set bonus.** Completing a set on one tool class: **+10%** to that set's skins
on that class. Eight sets, so a completionist caps at +10% on what they
actually use rather than a stacking global.

**No seasonal gate.** Halloween is a normal set and drops year-round like the
rest (owner, 2026-10-10). The only seasonal thing in the skin system is
nothing — the Candy Crypt's window governs the mine, not these.

## Open, and worth deciding before this is built

- **A 10× spread means a SSS skin on a mid-tier tool beats a plain top-tier
  tool.** That is the stated intent — the skin is the chase — but it inverts
  what progression means: ore tools become the floor. If that is too far, the
  same curve at **5×** (F 1.0 → SSS 5.0) keeps skins decisive without making
  the ore ladder cosmetic.
- **Tempers become skins** (owner, 2026-10-10). Anyone holding a temper needs
  migrating onto the nearest grade; that is a save migration, not a rename.
- **F is 40% of pulls and 1.0×**, i.e. no better than no skin at all. Either F
  needs a floor above 1.0, or F skins need to be worth owning for the look
  alone and the set bonus.
