# Skins — 200 concepts in 8 sets, plus a bonus set, F to SSS

**Status: PROPOSED (2026-10-10). Nothing is built.** This is the name/set/tier
roster for approval. Numbers and buffs are in the second half.

## How a skin works

- **200 concepts in the core eight sets**, plus a 25-strong **Halloween bonus
  set** that sits outside the count — 225 in all. Each concept exists for **all
  five tool classes**, same name, same set, same tier, same buffs, so 225
  designs is **1,125 skin instances**.
- **The skin is the damage BASE.** `damage = skinBase × oreDamage × everything else`.
  Ore tools are the floor; the skin is the ceiling and the chase.
- **Grades are the game's existing ladder** — `F D C B A S SS SSS`
  (`MineCards.GRADE_RANK`, rank 1–8). No E, because the game has no E.
- **The form may change per class, the identity may not.** A set's hammer can be
  an axe, its drill a laser, its explosive a canister — as long as it reads as
  the same object family. What must stay identical across the five is the
  palette, the material and the trim, because that is what makes a set
  recognisable in someone else's hands at twenty studs.

Per set: 25 concepts, weighted **F 6 · D 5 · C 4 · B 3 · A 3 · S 2 · SS 1 · SSS 1**.
One SSS per set — eight in the core sets, nine counting Halloween.

## Numbers — all PROPOSED

**Skin base multiplier.** This is the whole point of the system: it is the base
the ore tool multiplies, not a bonus bolted on after.

| F | D | C | B | A | S | SS | SSS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 | 1.3 | 1.7 | 2.2 | 3.0 | 4.2 | 6.5 | **10.0** |

**Pull rates from a chest skin case.**

| F | D | C | B | A | S | SS | SSS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 40% | 24% | 15% | 9% | 6% | 3.6% | 2.0% | **0.4%** |

**Set bonus.** Completing a set on one tool class: **+10%** to that set's skins
on that class. Eight sets, so a completionist caps at +10% on what they
actually use rather than a stacking global.

**No seasonal gate.** Halloween is a normal set and drops year-round like the
rest (owner, 2026-10-10). The only seasonal thing in the skin system is
nothing — the Candy Crypt's window governs the mine, not these.

## Open, and worth deciding before this is built

- **A 10× spread means a SSS skin on a mid-tier tool beats a plain top-tier
  tool.** That is the stated intent — the skin is the chase — but it inverts
  what progression means: ore tools become the floor. If that is too far, the
  same curve at **5×** (F 1.0 → SSS 5.0) keeps skins decisive without making
  the ore ladder cosmetic.
- **Tempers become skins** (owner, 2026-10-10). Anyone holding a temper needs
  migrating onto the nearest grade; that is a save migration, not a rename.
- **F is 40% of pulls and 1.0×**, i.e. no better than no skin at all. Either F
  needs a floor above 1.0, or F skins need to be worth owning for the look
  alone and the set bonus.
