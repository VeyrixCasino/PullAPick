# Drill icon sheet — generation prompt

100 drills on a 10×10 grid: the **82 ore drills** in tier order, then **18 Event
Horizon drills**. Colours below are the ore's real in-game `Color3` from
`MineConfig.ORES`, so an icon matches the block it comes from.

Paste everything between the rules into the image generator.

---

**A 10×10 grid of 100 mining drill icons, 256×256 px per cell, on a fully
transparent background (alpha, no checkerboard, no colour, no shadow catcher).
Each cell holds exactly one drill, centred, with a small even margin, and nothing
crossing between cells.**

**One silhouette, 100 finishes.** Every drill is the same base machine seen from
the same 3/4 angle: a stubby horizontal body with a grip at the back, a collar,
and a conical spiral bit pointing forward-left. Keep the proportions, the pose
and the lighting identical in all 100 cells. The ONLY things that change are
material, colour, surface detail and glow. This is a set, not 100 separate
designs — a player must recognise a drill instantly and then recognise *which*
drill by its colour.

**Read at 64 px.** These are inventory icons first. Strong single-colour
silhouette, high contrast against transparency, chunky forms, no thin filigree,
no text, no numbers, no borders, no rarity frames.

**Style.** Stylised game art for a Roblox game played mostly by kids: clean
bold shapes, saturated readable colour, soft rim light, a little specular on
metal. Not photoreal, not gritty, not muddy. The late-game drills should look
genuinely *wanted* — a kid should see the top row and decide to grind for it —
so push the glow, the energy and the exotic materials hard at the bottom of the
sheet while keeping the early ones humble and plain.

**Mixed materials are encouraged.** Most drills should read as two or three
colours working together — a body colour, a metal collar, and an accent on the
bit or the inscriptions — rather than one flat tint. Where an ore is pale, give
it a darker metal frame so it still reads on a light background.

## Notes for the 3D pass

These carry into the models, not just the icons:

- **Spectrite (73) must be a genuinely transparent mesh** — glass/crystal
  material with real transparency and refraction, not a painted-on look. Decide
  this at model time; it cannot be faked afterwards.
- **Umbrite (70)** needs an emissive mask for the purple seams so the black body
  stays black and only the seams light up.
- **Mythril (74)** inscriptions want to be an emissive texture layer over the
  base metal, so they can glow independently.
- **Uranium (78)** and the other five nuclear drills share one mesh with a
  swapped emissive colour — that is the cheapest way to keep the family reading
  as a family.
- The 18 Event Horizon drills share the base silhouette but want their own
  material set, since most of them are energy rather than metal.
