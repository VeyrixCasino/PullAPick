The mine stops at 5,000, not 10,000

MineConfig.LAYERS is assigned twice — 10,000 on line 15 and 5,000 on line 36. The later assignment wins, so the live ceiling is 5,000, which is also where the 75 MineDepth.SECTIONS stop.

MINE1_LAYERS still says 10,000 and MineDepth.SEAMS still lists 6000, 7000, 8000, 9000 and 10000. Those five are below the floor of the world: nothing generates there and no section prices them. Either the ceiling goes back to 10,000 and 75 more sections get authored, or those seams come out of the list.
