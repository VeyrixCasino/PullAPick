Ore Cabinet

All 121 ores as they now sit in the config, in tier order across the eight rarity bands. Colour, finish and glow are the real values the block spawns with. Say it with a tool shows how each name reads once a tool is made of it — which is the thing worth judging before any of these are locked in.

Tier sets difficulty as D = (tier − 1) × 0.2674, so tier 121 sits 32.09 steps of ×6 above tier 1 — the same unit the mine measures depth in. met and rough are the PBR values on the spawned part; glow ores carry a PointLight whose range follows its brightness.

33 of these are new to the roster. The other 88 kept their id, so saves holding them carry straight over; the retired 33 map to a replacement on load.
