FIXED — the stale divisor, and the curve rebuilt around it

toolTierPower read 6 ^ (TOOL_TIER_SPAN × (tier − 1) / 120). That 120 was #ORES − 1 when the roster held 121 ores; at 82 the exponent only ever reached 81/120 of its range, costing Exotic 11,127×. It now divides by ORE_COUNT − 1, so the two columns agree and the ratio is 1 by construction.

Fixing it alone would have made things worse, which is why it waited. It hands the overshoot to tier instead of level: the levels that still change a swing count drop from 23 to 8 out of 100. Two constants moved with it — TOOL_CLIMB_DAMAGE 3.9e8 → 350 and TOOL_TIER_SPAN 16 → 10.

350 is measured, not fitted: it is the HP growth across one zone's 5,000 layers (20 + 1.5×L, so 21.5 → 7,520), which is the span a tool's own levelling has to cover. A full climb is now worth exactly one zone of depth instead of 222× past the bottom of the mine.

Result: levels that still matter go from 24–30 per band to 55–99, and tier 60 → 82 at max level goes from 192× to 2,078×. Asserted against the live module by tools/verify/damage-curve.js.

Reach stops meaning anything at tier 67

Ore reach is tier + 15 capped at the roster. From tier 67 up that cap binds, so Legendary, Mythic, Divine and Exotic all reach exactly 82 — four bands with identical reach. Above Legendary, a better ore buys damage only; the progression gate you designed has already stopped moving.

Zap was the real offender

The old falloff was 0.9 per hop with a 32-hop cap, so at zap = 0.8 a chain still had a 38% chance to continue on hop nine. The cap was doing all the balancing and the falloff none. Now 0.75 on both chance and damage, six hops.

04

What is waiting on you

Four decisions, each one unblocks work

1 — the hat sheet above. Approve it, or name a target full-set multiplier and the curve gets fitted to it.

2 — the /120 divisor. DONE, together with the climb and the span, because fixing it on its own made levelling worse rather than better. The new open question is the per-band step (Legendary 2×, Mythic 4×, Divine 8×, Exotic 16×): a doubling per band is my pick, but the size of those four jumps is a feel question and therefore yours.

3 — EARTHQUAKE_SEC = 5. Still a guess, not your number. The share is 0.12/s, so the duration sets the whole quake at 0.60 of a swing.

4 — Blast rides oreYield, a key named for ore quantity doing proc chance. Renaming it is a save migration: low risk, not zero.
